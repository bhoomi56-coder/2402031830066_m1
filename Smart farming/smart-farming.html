About the Project

Revolutionizing Agriculture with Smart Technology

Smart farming integrates IoT sensors, cloud computing, and mobile technology to create an intelligent farming ecosystem — eliminating inefficiencies of traditional agriculture.

❌ Problems

  • 💦 Excessive water waste in manual irrigation
  • 🧑‍🌾 High manual labor costs
  • 😴 No 24/7 monitoring capability
  • 📉 Unpredictable crop yield

✅ Our Solution

  • 🤖 Automated precision irrigation
  • 📡 Remote monitoring via web/app
  • 📊 Real-time data analytics
  • 🔔 Smart alerts & notifications

Features

Everything Your Farm Needs

Our smart farming system comes packed with cutting-edge features that put complete farm control in the palm of your hand.

Core
🌱

Soil Moisture Detection

Capacitive sensors measure soil moisture at multiple depths in real-time, ensuring crops receive optimal hydration levels throughout the day.

Core
💧

Automatic Irrigation System

Smart pump control automatically waters crops when moisture drops below threshold — no manual intervention needed, 24/7.

Remote

Mobile & Web Dashboard

Temperature & Humidity Monitoring

DHT22 sensors continuously track ambient temperature and relative humidity, alerting you when conditions fall outside safe ranges.

Real-time Data Analytics

🔔

Alerts & Notifications

Receive instant push notifications when moisture is critically low, temperature spikes, or a system anomaly is detected in your farm.

How It Works

From Sensor to Action in 4 Steps

Our streamlined IoT pipeline ensures that farm data is collected, transmitted, analyzed, and acted upon in real-time — seamlessly.

1
🔌

Sensors Collect Data

Arduino/ESP8266 microcontrollers paired with soil moisture sensors, DHT22 temperature/humidity sensors, and light sensors continuously collect environmental data from the farm field every 30 seconds.

2
☁️

Data Sent to Cloud Server

Sensor readings are transmitted via Wi-Fi (ESP8266) to a Firebase/Node.js backend in real-time. Data is stored securely in the cloud and timestamped for historical analysis and trend detection.

3
📱

Farmer Checks Dashboard

The farmer accesses the real-time dashboard on any device — mobile, tablet, or desktop. Live gauges, charts, and alerts display all farm metrics at a glance, anywhere in the world.

4
🚿

Remote Control & Automation

With a single tap, the farmer turns irrigation pumps ON or OFF remotely. Alternatively, the system automatically activates irrigation when moisture falls below a preset threshold — smart farming on autopilot.
