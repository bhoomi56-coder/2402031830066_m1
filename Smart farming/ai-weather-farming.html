❌ The Problem

Sensors Are Not the Answer for Small Farmers

Over 80% of Indian farmers are small-scale, with less than 2 hectares of land. Sensor-based smart farming is simply out of reach for them.

High Hardware Cost

IoT sensors, Arduino boards, relay modules, and Wi-Fi adapters cost ₹10,000–₹20,000 per farm. Unaffordable for marginal farmers.

🔧

Constant Maintenance Needed

Soil sensors corrode, break, and need re-calibration every few months. Farmers need tech expertise they simply don't have.

📡

Poor Rural Connectivity

Real-time IoT data upload requires stable internet — unavailable or unaffordable in most rural agricultural regions of India.

🧑‍🌾

Technical Barrier

Setting up and operating sensor systems requires technical knowledge most farmers simply haven't had access to learn.

✅ Our Solution

Replace Sensors with Live Weather API

Free, real-time weather data from OpenWeatherMap is publicly available for every location in the world. We use it as our "virtual sensor" — for zero cost.

01

Detect User Location

Browser's Geolocation API automatically finds the farmer's GPS coordinates with one permission click.

02

Fetch Live Weather Data

Using fetch() API, we call OpenWeatherMap to get temperature, humidity, and rainfall in real-time.

03

Apply AI Prediction Logic

Our JavaScript rules engine processes the weather data and predicts soil moisture level and irrigation need instantly.

04

Display Smart Recommendation

Farmer sees a clear YES/NO decision with soil moisture status and crop-specific advice — no technical knowledge needed.

🤖 AI Decision Rules
💧
IRRIGATE: If humidity < 40% AND rainfall = 0mm
→ Soil moisture is LOW → Irrigation REQUIRED
🚫
SKIP: If humidity > 70% OR rainfall > 0mm
→ Soil has enough moisture → No irrigation needed
⚠️
MONITOR: Humidity 40–70%, rainfall = 0mm
→ Moderate moisture → Check again in 3–4 hours
