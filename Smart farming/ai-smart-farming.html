❌ The Problem

Traditional Sensors Are a Barrier for Small Farmers

Millions of small-scale farmers in India cannot afford IoT hardware. We need a smarter, cheaper solution that uses data — not devices.

VS

Our AI approach vs. sensor-based systems

💸

High Hardware Cost

Soil moisture sensors, IoT modules, and relay systems cost ₹3,000–₹15,000+ per farm setup — unaffordable for marginal farmers earning < ₹10,000/month.

🔧

Complex Maintenance

Sensors degrade in soil, corrode with moisture, and require technical expertise to calibrate and replace — creating ongoing dependency on technicians.

📵

Poor Connectivity in Rural Areas

Many farming regions lack stable internet or electricity, making real-time IoT data transmission unreliable or impossible to sustain.

🧑‍🌾

Low Digital Literacy

Small farmers aren't trained in hardware setup or app management. A sensor system without support becomes useless within weeks of deployment.

✅ Our Solution

ML on Historical Data Instead of Sensors

We replace physical sensors with intelligence. By training machine learning models on years of agricultural data, we can predict what sensors would measure — for free.

Our AI Pipeline

01
Collect Historical Data

Gather 5+ years of rainfall, temp, humidity, and soil readings from govt agricultural databases.

02
Clean & Preprocess

Handle missing values, normalize features, encode crop types, split into train/test sets.

03
Train ML Models

Apply Linear Regression for moisture prediction, Decision Tree for irrigation classification.

04
Predict & Recommend

Input today's weather → get predicted soil moisture + irrigation advice in real-time.

Process

How the AI System Works

Four clean steps from raw historical data to an intelligent, actionable irrigation recommendation for any farmer in India.

01
📦

Collect Past Data

Gather rainfall, temperature, humidity, and soil moisture records from government databases (IMD, ICAR) spanning 5+ years for the target region.

02
🧠

Train ML Model

Feed cleaned data into Linear Regression and Decision Tree algorithms. The model learns patterns between weather conditions and soil moisture levels.

03
⌨️

Input Today's Weather

Farmer inputs current temperature, humidity, and rainfall (available free from any weather app or government weather station without any sensor).

04
✅

Get Smart Prediction

The trained model instantly predicts soil moisture % and whether irrigation is needed — with crop-specific advice and next 5-day irrigation plan.
